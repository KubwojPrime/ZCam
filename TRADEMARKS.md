## Forks And Modified Versions

Forks and modified versions must not present themselves as the official ZCam
project unless they are distributed by KubwojPrime or have explicit written
permission from KubwojPrime.

Forks should use a clearly distinct app name, package-visible branding, icon,
and store listing identity. A fork may say that it is based on ZCam, but it
must not imply endorsement, official status, or upstream control.

Examples of acceptable naming:

- `ExampleCam, based on ZCam`
- `ExampleCam, a fork of ZCam`

Examples of unacceptable naming without written permission:

- `ZCam Pro`
- `ZCam Plus`
- `Official ZCam`
- `ZCam by <other publisher>`
